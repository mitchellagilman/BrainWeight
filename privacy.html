Privacy policy

Effective November 10, 2026

The short version: we collect what the app needs to work: your account, your training, what you post and your messages. We don’t sell your information, we don’t show ads, and we don’t use tracking or advertising tools. You can delete your account and data from inside the app at any time.

This policy explains how Brainweight (“Brainweight,” “we,” “us”) handles information when you use the Brainweight app and website at brainweight.fit (the “Service”).

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address, password (stored only in scrambled, hashed form), username, display name, and optional profile photo and bio.
  • Training data: workouts, sets, weights and reps, routines, custom exercises, plans you build (including answers like your goal and a best recent set), settings and rest timer preferences.
  • Brain game scores: the scores you earn, used for your progress charts and leaderboards.
  • Social activity: posts, photos and videos you upload, comments, likes, follows, follow requests, blocks, reports and direct messages.
  • Subscription details: your plan, its status and renewal date, and a customer ID from our payment provider. We never see or store your card number. Payments are handled by Stripe.
  • Notification settings: if you turn on notifications, a device address that lets us send alerts to your phone, plus your reminder days, time and time zone.
  • Invites and promo codes: which code you used and who invited you, so rewards go to the right people.
  • Basic technical information: our hosting providers keep standard logs (like IP address, browser type and time of request) to keep the Service running and secure.

Some data, like your workouts, settings and best game scores, is also saved on your device so the app works quickly and offline.

How we use it

  • To run the Service: sign you in, save and sync your training, show your feed, deliver messages and send the notifications you ask for.
  • To build plans, charts, streaks and leaderboards from your own data.
  • To process subscriptions, free trials, promo codes and invite rewards.
  • To send account emails, like sign-up confirmation and password resets. We don’t send marketing emails without asking.
  • To keep the community safe: review reports, enforce our terms and community guidelines, and prevent abuse.
  • To understand overall use (for example, how many people worked out this week) using counts, not individual tracking.

Who can see what

  • Your profile (name, username, photo and bio) is visible to other signed-in members.
  • Your posts are visible to signed-in members. If you make your account private, only followers you approve can see them. Coach accounts are always public.
  • Direct messages are visible only to the people in the conversation. Messages are protected in transit but aren’t end-to-end encrypted, so technical administrators could access them if needed for safety, legal reasons or to fix a problem.
  • Leaderboards show your username, photo and best score to members who view them.
  • Your training data, plans and settings are private to you unless you choose to share a workout or lift.

Companies that help us run Brainweight

We share information only with service providers that help run the Service, and only as needed:

  • Supabase: our database, sign-in system, file storage and server functions.
  • Stripe: subscription payments.
  • Resend: account emails.
  • GitHub Pages: hosts the app’s website files.
  • Google Fonts: provides the app’s fonts, which means Google receives your IP address when fonts load.
  • Apple, Google or Mozilla push services: deliver notifications to your device if you turn them on.

We don’t sell your personal information, and we don’t share it for advertising. We may disclose information if required by law, to protect someone’s safety, or as part of a sale or transfer of the business (in which case this policy would continue to apply).

How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is open. When you delete your account (Profile → settings → Delete account), we delete your account, profile, workouts, posts, photos, videos, comments, messages and scores, and cancel any subscription. Copies may remain in backups for a short time before they’re overwritten. Payment records kept by Stripe follow Stripe’s own retention rules.

Your choices and rights

  • Edit your profile, make your account private, control who can message you, and turn notifications on or off in the app.
  • Delete your account and data at any time from inside the app.
  • Ask us for a copy of your information, or ask us to correct or delete it, by emailing us. Depending on where you live (for example, California or the EU), you may have additional rights. We’ll honor them.

Children

Brainweight isn’t for children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect information from them. People aged 13 to 17 may use Brainweight only with a parent or guardian’s permission. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, contact us and we’ll delete it.

Security

Data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS), passwords are hashed, and database rules limit each person to their own private data. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

Changes

If we make important changes to this policy, we’ll update the date above and let you know in the app.

Contact

Questions or requests: support@brainweight.fit. You can also message the Brainweight coach account inside the app.
